Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) Panel
Switches capacitor stages in response to changing reactive power demand.
Auto Mains Failure Panel
Automatically manages the transition between mains power and a standby generator.
Auto Change Over Panel
Transfers a load between two available electrical sources.
Air Insulated Rising Main
Routes electrical power vertically through a building using enclosed busbars.
DOL Panels
Starts compatible motors directly across the supply using a contactor and overload protection.
Control Desk
Brings process controls, instruments and status displays to an operator station.
Feeder Pillar
Provides a local distribution point for multiple outgoing circuits.
HVAC Panels
Centralizes control and protection for air-handling, ventilation and cooling equipment.
Instrumentation Panel
Groups field signal interfaces, indicating instruments and monitoring devices.
Main LT Panels
Receives and distributes low-voltage power at the main electrical intake.
Motor Control Center
Groups motor feeders, starters and protective devices in one assembly.
PLC Panel
Houses programmable logic controllers, input and output modules, and associated power supplies.
Power Distribution Board
Distributes electrical power to branch circuits with individually protected outgoing ways.
Power Control Centre
Consolidates incoming supply, metering and outgoing feeders for low-voltage power distribution.
Star Delta Panel
Reduces starting current for compatible three-phase motors using staged switching.
VFD Panels
Houses variable frequency drives for applications that need adjustable motor speed.
HT Panels
Switches and protects incoming high-tension circuits in suitable distribution systems.
LT Panels
Controls and distributes low-tension electrical power across downstream loads.
Bus Duct & Accessories
Carries power through enclosed rigid conductors between equipment or distribution points.
Meter Board
Groups meters and associated connections for centralized energy monitoring.
Thyristor Switching Panel
Uses solid-state switching for applications that need rapid switching response.
Cable Tray & Accessories
Supports and organizes electrical cable runs through a facility.
Smart Panels
Combines power distribution with status monitoring and communication options.
PCC Panel
Configures a low-voltage power control assembly around the incoming sources, bus coupler and outgoing feeder schedule.
Drive Panel
Integrates drive controls and protective devices for motor-driven machinery.
Distribution Panel
Feeds downstream circuits in a single assembly with protection and isolation for each outgoing load.
MCC Panel
Coordinates motor starters, local and remote controls, and overload protection for a group of motors.
