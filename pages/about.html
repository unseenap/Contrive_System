Answers to the questions that come before a panel enquiry.

What should I include in a panel enquiry?

Share the application, supply voltage, load schedule, installation location and delivery timeline. Add a single-line diagram or equipment list when available.

How do I choose a panel family?

Start with the load. MCC and VFD panels serve motor applications, PCC and distribution panels handle power distribution, and PLC panels bring process controls together. Explore each product page for the details to include in your brief.

Can I enquire about an existing installation?

Yes. Describe the existing panel, the change you are planning and any available drawings. Include the existing ratings, connected loads and space constraints.

How do I save the product catalogue?
